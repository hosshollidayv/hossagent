Governance by design

Instrument the workflow. Protect the operator.

Collection is purpose-limited, pseudonymous by default, locally governed, and prohibited from individual personnel ranking.

Collect

Workflow state, release exposure, adjudicated outcome, and defined guardrails.

Minimize

No raw keystrokes, screen replay, payload content, or precise location by default.

Separate

The recorded UI action is not treated as proof that the task was completed correctly.

Retain authority

HossAgent recommends. The named mission, test, and product owners decide.
