Signal intelligence becomes a reviewable opportunity flow.

HossAgent is organized around a single operational sequence. Public signals enter the system, context is extracted, relevance is assessed, enrichment fills in the target, and outbound is queued for customer review before anything is sent.

01

SignalNet monitors public business movement

Job posts, news, permits, competitor shifts, reviews, and other market changes enter as structured signals.

02

ArticleStorm captures source context

Each signal keeps its original excerpt, source link, and extracted payload so the user can see where it came from.

03

BaselineRecall explains whether it matters

The system records why the event should matter now, which verticals it maps to, and what changed commercially.

04

ARCHANGEL and HMX enrich the target

Company and contact data are expanded into domains, phone confidence, email confidence, and enrichment state.

05

Outbound Review controls the send path

The customer can approve, edit, or skip the draft and the application persists the choice locally.

Stage 1Signal intake
Stage 2Context extraction
Stage 3Reasoning and scoring
Stage 4Enrichment and confidence
Stage 5Review and send control
The workflow is designed so backend modules can be swapped and improved without changing the user experience.
