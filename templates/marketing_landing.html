How it works

A clear path from observation to judgment.

  1. 01Observe

    Collect traceable evidence from the operating environment.

  2. 02Normalize

    Turn scattered inputs into a consistent evidence model.

  3. 03Reason

    Apply the correct decision and consequence logic.

  4. 04Recommend

    Explain the next action, blockers, and no-go conditions.

  5. 05Operator decides

    The operator approves, redirects, or stops the motion.

Operating principles

Automation should sharpen judgment—not replace it.

HossAgent is designed around explicit controls that keep recommendations honest, explainable, and ready for operator review.

  • 01Operator approval required
  • 02Evidence retains provenance
  • 03Guardrails outrank speed
  • 04Recommendations show limitations
  • 05Decision engines remain mission-specific
