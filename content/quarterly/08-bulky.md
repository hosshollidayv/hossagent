Title: Bulky Baggage
Date: 2026-10-02
Category: Arrival dispatches
Deck: Original Log 13A: the instrument was fine. The owner required recalibration.

The Telecaster was with the pets.

Not lost. Not damaged. Not beginning an unauthorized European tour. It had been delivered to bulky baggage, where an airport employee had evidently concluded that an American guitar belonged among the kennels and other unusually shaped responsibilities.

This was a reasonable classification. The Telecaster is spiritually closer to a working dog than a designer suitcase. Reliable, uncomplicated, willing to go almost anywhere, and not particularly concerned with how the trip photographs.

The case was intact. The guitar was intact. My concern had been sincere, but its entire operational premise was now standing a short walk away, perfectly unharmed.

There is a special embarrassment in discovering that every inanimate object involved in an incident has handled it better than you have. The guitar had done nothing wrong. The case had done nothing wrong. Even the airport had delivered the instrument to a place where instruments were delivered.

Only I had begun drafting an internal diplomatic protest.

With the equipment accounted for, the baggage hall reverted to being an ordinary room with belts. The same place that had seemed capable of swallowing my future now merely needed an exit sign.

Lesson recorded: before declaring a guitar missing, check where they put the dogs.
