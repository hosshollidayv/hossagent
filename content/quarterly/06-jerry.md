Title: Jerry, We Meet Again
Date: 2026-10-02
Category: Arrival dispatches
Deck: After months of preparation, Germany becomes an actual floor beneath your shoes.

After months of emails, orders, bookings, forms, and the kind of preparation that begins to feel like a substitute for travel, I was standing in Frankfurt Airport. Germany had ceased to be a duty location in a correspondence chain. It was now the place containing my feet.

Immigration was complete. The next objective was baggage, humanity's preferred method of concluding a technological miracle. Four engines and an international navigation system carry you across the Atlantic; a rubber belt then invites you to reconsider your confidence in civilization.

I had brought enough equipment to suggest that the Army had assigned a small touring act. The Martin had traveled in the cabin. The Telecaster had entered the system. I was waiting for the system to return it.

This was a return to Germany, but not a return to being the person who had lived here before. I was older, had different responsibilities, and had left a family at home. Familiar signs and sounds were arriving in the company of an unfamiliar absence.

There would be time to think about that. At baggage claim, the task remained mercifully concrete: find the bags, find the guitar, find the ride.

A year is a large thing to contemplate. A rectangular guitar case is an excellent place to start.
