Title: The Guitars Have Landed
Date: 2026-10-02
Category: Arrival dispatches
Deck: Original Log 15: arrival becomes real when the first chord sounds right.

The hotel door closed. The bags were inside. I opened the cases.

The Telecaster emerged from its checked-baggage adventure looking exactly as it had before: magnificently pre-damaged. There is a practical advantage to owning a heavily reliced guitar. A pristine instrument invites forensic investigation after every journey. This one would require an archaeological team to identify a new mark.

The Martin offered a different kind of reassurance. It had decades of actual use behind it. I took it out, played a chord, and found that it was still in tune.

After the airport, the flight, the overhead bin, the arrival, and the drive, the old guitar had retained its position more consistently than I had retained mine.

It also sounded wonderful. Better, I thought, although I cannot separate the room's acoustics from the relief of hearing something familiar in it. Guitar owners routinely turn this kind of uncertainty into a theory about wood. I am capable of that, too. For the moment, I simply played another chord.

The equipment was safe. The next task was to call home.

That call required a shorter report than the journey had generated. Everyone was here. Everything had arrived. Dad was in Germany.

The baggage inventory proved that the travel was finished. The sound of the Martin made it feel that way.
