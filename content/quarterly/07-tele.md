Title: Where Is My Telecaster?
Date: 2026-10-02
Category: Arrival dispatches
Deck: Original Log 13: a missing five percent takes command of the entire operation.

Everything arrived. Almost.

“Almost” is a hostile word at baggage claim. Two luggage carts stood beside me carrying nearly everything required for a year abroad. The missing item was the Telecaster, which immediately became more important than the entire visible inventory.

If no luggage arrives, you have a delay. If every bag except one arrives, you have a personal relationship with uncertainty. Each departing passenger seems to take a little of your reassurance along with his suitcase.

I had spent considerable time explaining that the Telecaster was nearly indestructible. This was proving irrelevant to whether I could locate it. A plank with excellent structural integrity is still difficult to play from another country.

The Martin, safely beside me, had the air of an instrument whose travel arrangements had been made by a more competent adult.

I began considering the special-baggage area. The distinction mattered: I had not established that the guitar was lost. I had established that it was not where I was looking. These are different findings, although the nervous system is happy to treat them as equivalent.

Somewhere in Frankfurt Airport was a rectangle containing six strings and an unreasonable share of my emotional equilibrium. The mission had become extremely specific.
