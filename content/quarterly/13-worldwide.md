Title: Mr. Worldwide
Date: 2026-10-03
Category: After hours
Deck: Original Log 18: a Mainz bar provides an unexpected assessment of celebrity intelligence.

In Mainz, I entered a bar, ordered beer, and met people. After several days of forms, transport, and verification, the simplicity was refreshing. No onboarding flow. A conversation began because we were sitting near one another.

One of the men, Benedict, spoke German at a speed compatible with my comprehension. This immediately elevated him in my personal hierarchy of useful people. His friend arrived. A guessing game followed.

I was assigned a celebrity and had to identify myself through questions. The answer was Pitbull. The clues, as I understood them, had done very little to assist with this discovery.

Apparently I was a singer but not a rapper. My search moved confidently away from the correct answer. Benedict somehow reached it anyway. Either he possessed exceptional celebrity knowledge, or we were operating with different filing systems for American popular culture.

I accepted Mr. Worldwide as an evening appointment. An American from the Miami area, newly arrived in Germany and struggling to identify a Miami musician in a German bar: the casting had a certain economy.

There was briefly a suggestion that I play something. My guitars were elsewhere, having already completed their international duties. The proposal subsided.

I left and wandered through the old town toward another brewery. For once, the route did not need to be optimized. The point was being there, with pockets instead of luggage, learning a place through an evening that had not required a plan.
