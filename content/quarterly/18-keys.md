Title: The Keys Were Uphill
Date: 2026-10-05
Category: Field notes
Deck: The administrative campaign briefly becomes a walk back to the hotel.

I had places to be, paperwork to carry, and a schedule that appeared to account for the movement of a functioning adult. I had also left my backpack and car keys at the hotel.

The hotel was uphill. This detail had not been significant when the day was planned. It became the dominant feature of the revised plan.

There is something clarifying about a mistake this ordinary. No disputed policy. No opaque status. No institutional design problem waiting to be explained. The keys were where I had left them. The solution involved walking.

I went back.

In-processing encourages the illusion that the day's difficulty resides entirely in the organization. There are offices, documents, opening hours, and the occasional appointment whose relationship with the closing time invites interpretation. It is comforting to believe that a better system would remove every obstacle.

A better system would not have put my keys in my pocket that morning. I would have had to participate.

The walk was therefore a useful contribution to the accuracy of the record. Bureaucracy had plenty to answer for. This particular delay belonged to the author.

I recovered the bag, recovered the keys, and continued. The day's plan had survived contact with its least predictable component: the man carrying it.
