Title: Up the Neroberg
Date: 2026-10-04
Category: After hours
Deck: Original Log 21: a hill delivers exactly the difficulty advertised.

I ran from the hotel toward the Neroberg. The hill was immediately clear about its terms.

After airports, beer, hotel rooms, and repeated encounters with systems whose status required interpretation, it was refreshing to meet a gradient. The hill was not awaiting approval. It had no temporary outage. Its service consisted of being uphill, continuously, and it delivered.

The gold domes of the Russian church appeared along the way. This is one of the advantages of running in a European city: you set out to improve your circulation and encounter architecture that would justify its own trip. I slowed down, partly for appreciation and partly because appreciation had become convenient.

Higher up, Wiesbaden opened below me. Streets I had experienced as separate problems began to form a place.

At the war memorial, the dates interrupted the exercise. Five years carved into stone can weigh more than their typography suggests. I stood there for a moment, an American officer in running clothes, looking at another generation's military dead.

Then I continued. The descent restored my confidence until my knees submitted their opinion.

I made it up and back. That was the measure. No elaborate interpretation required.

The city felt less unfamiliar afterward. I had seen its shape and crossed a small part of it under my own power. The assignment was still a year long. The evening was simply an evening, and I was properly tired.
