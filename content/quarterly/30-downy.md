Title: Downy Beads and the Grand Tour of Errands
Date: 2026-10-10
Category: After hours
Deck: Adapted from Log 29: a new car, laundry supplies, and the scale of an ordinary Saturday.

The Škoda went back. The Volvo entered the story. A vehicle exchange has a satisfyingly definite shape: return one set of keys, receive another, and immediately begin learning where a different manufacturer believes the controls belong.

Other errands followed. Arrangements for the apartment. A stop for cash. The exchange. At one point, three bottles of Downy products had become part of the day's inventory.

This is the part of international relocation that resists cinematic treatment. Nobody sends the camera crew for the laundry supplies. Yet this is where the move becomes real. You are no longer packing for a trip. You are buying things that assume you will keep doing laundry here.

The laundry supplies were admirably concrete. Three bottles. Physically present. No pending signature and no need to establish which department owned the next action. In an unusually complicated week, they had achieved a level of certainty that I respected.

There was food. There was movement across town. There were conversations whose subjects wandered well beyond the practical purpose of the errand. A Saturday can contain an impressive range of human concerns while still ending with the discovery that one more task remains.

I had a new car and a growing collection of domestic objects. These were small proofs of commitment to a place.

The grand narrative was that I had returned to Germany for a year. The immediate narrative was that I was carrying laundry supplies. Both were accurate. The second was what needed to get through the door.
