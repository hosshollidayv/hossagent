Title: The Unsexy Workflow
Date: 2026-10-08
Category: Systems & people
Deck: Original Log 27: the future arrives one corrected field at a time.

There are days when the work can be described with words like strategy. There are other days when the work is correcting a field on a form because the field is wrong.

October offered a generous supply of the second kind.

Housing arrangements were progressing. A handover was becoming a real appointment. Other administrative steps still depended on a document, a person, an office, or a clarification that had not yet reached the place where it was needed. Somewhere in this collection was lunch, an event capable of suspending an apparently imminent transaction without changing its official status.

The work was unsexy. It was also the work.

In product conversations, we tend to favor the moment when an idea becomes a feature. Less attention goes to the moment when a person discovers which version of a document is required, who receives it, and what constitutes a usable submission. That moment can determine whether the feature ever matters.

My own day kept reducing to specific actions. Correct the field. Confirm the arrangement. Clarify the rule. Make sure the next person has what is needed. None of these tasks became more useful when described at a higher altitude.

A conversation about software and workflows belonged in the same day. The material was all around me. It did not require a speculative future in which organizations suddenly developed coordination problems. The present had provided several.

The discipline was to understand them before proposing anything. A workflow is a sequence of consequences experienced by people. Calling it a workflow should not permit us to forget either the consequences or the people.

The form was corrected. Another step could proceed. There are worse definitions of progress.
