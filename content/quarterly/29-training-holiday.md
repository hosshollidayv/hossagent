Title: Training Holiday, My Ass
Date: 2026-10-10
Category: Field notes
Deck: Original Log 28: a calendar designation meets the unfinished business of moving abroad.

A training holiday is an attractive phrase. It implies that the institution has paused and that a person associated with the institution might reasonably pause with it.

The apartment, utility arrangements, and remaining move-in tasks had apparently not received the same notice.

I spent the available time moving those things along. There were questions to resolve, arrangements to confirm, and the practical business of making a new address habitable. The word “holiday” remained on the calendar, functioning primarily as an editorial comment.

This is a recognizable feature of a move abroad. The major event is crossing an ocean. The smaller events then occupy every available surface. A key. A service. An account. A piece of information somebody needs before the next thing can happen. The distance traveled is enormous; the final obstacles are often measured in fields.

I also found time to think about an amplifier. This does not weaken my complaint about being busy. It establishes that the complaint was being made by a complete human being, one whose attention occasionally escaped the administrative perimeter and went looking for tweed.

The point of finishing the errands was to recover a portion of ordinary life. A room you can settle into. A place for the guitars. Fewer unresolved arrangements accompanying you to breakfast.

By the end of such a day, the official designation matters less than whether something became easier. I wanted the following week to contain less arrival and more living.

The calendar had offered a holiday. I used it to negotiate with Monday.
