Title: Successful Day, Mostly
Date: 2026-10-03
Category: Arrival dispatches
Deck: Original Log 17: the map begins acquiring people.

A helpful civilian employee explained what happened next. Dates appeared. Places acquired names. The next couple of weeks stopped being an administrative cloud and began to resemble a calendar.

This is an underrated kind of help. A newcomer often has plenty of information and very little orientation. Someone who can distinguish the essential step from the surrounding ceremony saves more than time; she makes the situation intelligible.

I also met my sponsor over dinner. Until then, he had existed as messages and useful advice. Across a table, with beer and food between us, the arrangement became a relationship. There is still no satisfactory digital replacement for discovering that the person helping you is a good person to have dinner with.

Transportation improved when I remembered that Uber existed. An absent shuttle had consumed a disproportionate amount of thought. A car arrived and reduced the problem to a ride.

I chose a vehicle subscription and began looking more seriously at housing. The phone remained a project. Germany was celebrating its unity; my devices had not yet achieved theirs.

The day was successful in the ordinary, qualified way real days are successful. Some things worked. Some required help. Others could wait.

The important change was that I was making choices again. Yesterday I had mostly been cargo. Today I was beginning to operate.
